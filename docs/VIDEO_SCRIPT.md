# Round 1 demo video (under 3 minutes)

## The easy way: let autoplay drive

1. Open the demo in Chrome, full screen, at 1440 x 900 or larger. Zoom 100%.
2. Start your screen recorder (OBS, Xbox Game Bar with Win+Alt+R, or QuickTime on a Mac).
3. Press **Play the full demo** in the guide. It resets, logs in and plays 14 scenes with captions in about 2 min 20 s.
4. Stop recording when the last caption says "Team Silicon Knights".

That leaves about 30 seconds for a spoken intro or outro if you want one. Keep the whole video under 3:00.
