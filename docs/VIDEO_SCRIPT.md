# Round 1 demo video (under 3 minutes)

## The easy way: let autoplay drive

1. Open the demo in Chrome at 1440 x 900 or larger, zoom 100%, full screen (F11).
2. Pick the language with the **English / বাংলা** switch in the top bar. The captions follow it.
3. Start the screen recorder (OBS; or `Win+Alt+R` on Windows; or GNOME's `Ctrl+Shift+Alt+R` on Linux).
4. Press **Play the full demo**. It resets the records, switches between the five accounts and plays 13 scenes with captions in a little over two minutes.
5. Stop recording when the last caption says "Team Silicon Knights".

Direct links: `/?lang=bn&autoplay=1` or `/?lang=en&autoplay=1` start it on load.

That leaves about 40 seconds for a spoken intro or outro. Keep the whole video under 3:00.

## If you record by hand instead

Follow the **Walkthrough** tab in order and press **Take me there** on each step. Keep each step to about 12 seconds. Open the **API log** tab once for 3 seconds on the clearance call, to show the calls are real.

For the Owner Lock moment, the strongest shot is two devices: press **Use a real phone**, scan the code with a phone, and film the SMS arriving on it when scenario 4 runs.
